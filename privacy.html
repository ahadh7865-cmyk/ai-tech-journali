Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2025 • Effective Immediately

At AI Tech Journal (accessible via our publication domains), the privacy and security of our readers are of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy document outlines the types of personal information that is collected and recorded by AI Tech Journal and how we utilize it.

1. Information We Collect

We believe in minimal data footprint architectures. When you visit our static site, we may record standard non-personally identifiable log information:

  • Server Log Files: Like standard web hosts, our hosting infrastructure logs visitor access (IP addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), timestamp, referring/exit pages, and click counts). These are not linked to personally identifiable information and are used solely for analyzing trends, administering the site, and preventing DDoS attacks.
  • Newsletter Subscriptions: If you voluntarily subscribe to our weekly newsletter, we collect only your email address. You may unsubscribe at any time via the one-click unsubscribe link provided in every dispatch.
  • Contact Inquiries: If you submit a query via our contact form, your name, email, and message content will be retained temporarily to facilitate our correspondence.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

AI Tech Journal uses minimal local storage to remember user preferences (such as search history and notification dismissals). Third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites.

3. Google AdSense & Third-Party Advertising Disclosures

We may partner with third-party advertising networks such as Google AdSense to support our independent journalism:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. GDPR Compliance (General Data Protection Regulation)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you have certain data protection rights:

  • The right to access, update, or delete the information we have on you.
  • The right of rectification.
  • The right to object to processing.
  • The right to data portability.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@aitechjournal.com.

5. CCPA Privacy Rights (California Consumer Privacy Act)

Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to request disclosure of categories of personal data collected, request deletion of collected data, and request that a business that sells personal data not sell the consumer's personal data. AI Tech Journal does not sell personal information.

6. Children's Information

AI Tech Journal does not knowingly collect any Personal Identifiable Information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that your child provided this kind of information on our website, we strongly encourage you to contact us immediately and we will promptly remove such information from our records.
